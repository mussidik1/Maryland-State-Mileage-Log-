Add it to your Home Screen first

It works like an app from there. On iPhone, the Home Screen version keeps its own copy of your log, so set it up there.

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari.
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen (scroll down if you don’t see it), then Add.
  3. Open Mileage Log from your Home Screen.
